Italian Pizza:

Italian Pizza is a famous Italian dish made with a crispy crust, tomato sauce, mozzarella cheese, and fresh toppings. It is easy to make and loved by people all over the world.

Ingredients

Steps
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 teaspoon sugar
  • 1 packet active dry yeast
  • 3/4 cup warm water
  • 2 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1/2 cup tomato sauce
  • 1 cup shredded mozzarella
  • Fresh basil leaves
  • Oregano
  • Black pepper
  • Cherry tomatoes (optional)
  • Mushrooms
  • Bell peppers
  • Olives (optional)
  1. Mix the warm water, sugar, and yeast in a bowl. Let it rest for 5–10 minutes.
  2. Add the flour, salt, and olive oil. Mix well to form a soft dough.
  3. Knead the dough for about 8–10 minutes until smooth.
  4. Cover the dough and let it rise for 1 hour.
  5. Preheat the oven to 220°C (425°F).
  6. Roll out the dough into a round pizza base.
  7. Spread the tomato sauce evenly over the dough.
  8. Add mozzarella cheese and your favorite toppings.
  9. Sprinkle oregano and black pepper on top.
  10. Bake for 12–15 minutes until the crust is golden and the cheese is melted.
  11. Remove the pizza from the oven and let it cool for 2–3 minutes.
  12. Slice the pizza and serve hot. Enjoy your homemade Italian pizza!

Pro Tips:

  • Always use fresh mozzarella cheese for the best flavor.
  • Let the pizza dough rise properly for a soft and airy crust.
  • Do not overload the pizza with toppings to keep the crust crispy.
  • Preheat the oven before baking for even cooking.
  • Garnish with fresh basil leaves after baking for an authentic Italian taste.
